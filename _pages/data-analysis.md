In February 2025 I joined a research project with the Faculty of Medicine & Dentistry
to contribute data analysis and visualizations for a study on pain assessment in dementia
patients.

I co-authored a research article and a poster presented at the Canadian Conference on Dementia.
I analyzed survey data from different medical specialties using SPSS and Excel, identifying
patterns in how pain is managed across specialties and uncovering areas of agreement and
disagreement among providers.

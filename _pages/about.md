I'm Abbas Rizvi, a third-year Computer Science student at the
[University of Alberta](https://www.ualberta.ca/) with a minor in Mathematics

where I published a first-author paper at IEEE BIBE 2025 on cardiac MRI segmentation
using transfer learning.

Outside of school and work, I enjoy reading, colouring, and learning
about history and science.

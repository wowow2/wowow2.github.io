In May 2025 I joined the [Servier Virtual Cardiac Centre](https://spaces.facsci.ualberta.ca/svcc/)
to research cardiac MRI segmentation and ventricular volume estimation under the supervision of
Dr. Kumaradevan Punithakumar and Dr. Michelle Noga.

I am first author on a paper published at IEEE BIBE 2025,
where we benchmarked deep learning models (nnUNet, pretrained models, and our proposed transfer
learning approach) for right ventricular MRI segmentation — our approach achieved the best results.

[Read the paper (PDF)](/assets/IEEE_BIBE2025.pdf)

I also work on reducing the number of MRI slices needed to compute accurate right ventricular volume.
Our baseline integrates over all short-axis slices; our approaches range from naive slice reduction
to using long-axis slice diameters in regression models (linear regression and gradient boosting)
applied to clinician-reviewed data.

Currently I am a [MITACS](https://www.mitacs.ca/) intern on the project with
[Chromacare Labs](https://chromacarelabs.com/).

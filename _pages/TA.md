During the Fall 2025 semester I worked as a Teaching Assistant for CMPUT 201: Practical
Programming Methodology, a course that teaches C programming, version control with Git,
and competence with the Linux terminal.

I led weekly lab sections where students' assignments were evaluated and students could get
help with their current work. I helped develop assignments and in-class coding exercises,
from formulating ideas to writing sample solutions. I also proctored exams and answered
course-related questions on forums and via email, providing detailed feedback to help students
improve their programming and problem-solving skills.

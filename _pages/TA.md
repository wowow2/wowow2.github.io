During the Fall 2025 semester, I worked as a Teaching Assistant for CMPUT 201. The course is titled "Practial Programming Methodology"; it teaches C programming, use of version control, and builds competence with the linux terminal. My responsibilties included leading weekly lab sections where students assignments were evaulated, and students could get help with their current assignments. I also helped develop the assignments and in class coding exercises, which ranged from formulating ideas to writing sample solutions. I helped proctor exams, as well as answer any general course related questions students had in forums or via email.
